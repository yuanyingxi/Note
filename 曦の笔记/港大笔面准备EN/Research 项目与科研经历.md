# 1 Could you tell me more about your research project?

**英文题目：**

Could you tell me more about your research project?

**中文题目：**

可以详细介绍一下你的科研项目吗？

**English Answer:**

One of my research projects foucus on evaluating the robustness of Visual Question Answering models under low-quality image conditions.

I compared different visual and text encoders within the same framework to see how they perform on low-quality images.

I also tested the models on synthetic image distortions and real-world image quality problems to compare whether their results are the same.

In addition, I tested different data augmentation strategies to see whether they could improve model performance and robustness.

**中文参考回答：**

我的科研项目主要研究低质量图像条件下视觉问答模型的鲁棒性。

我主要在统一的特征融合框架下，对比了不同视觉编码器和文本编码器的表现，观察它们在图像质量较差时的性能差异。

我还从两种角度评估模型：一种是合成图像退化，另一种是 VizWiz-VQA 数据集中真实存在的图像质量问题，并对比他们是否一致。

此外，我也测试了不同的数据增强策略，分析它们能否提升模型的性能和鲁棒性。

# 2. What was the main challenge in your research?

**英文题目：**

What was the main challenge in your research?

**中文题目：**

你在科研过程中遇到的主要挑战是什么？

**English Answer:**  

One of the main challenges was that I wanted to explore how different types of distortions affect the model's robustness, but synthetic image distortions can't fully capture the complex distortions in the real world. So, I compared the model's performance on both synthetic data and the real-world images from VIWIZ, to see if they would lead to similar conclusions.

**中文参考回答：**  
我遇到的主要挑战之一是，我想探究不同类型的失真对模型鲁棒性的影响，但合成图像失真无法完全捕捉真实世界中复杂的失真情况。因此，我对比了模型在合成数据和 VIWIZ 真实图片上的表现，看看这两种评估方式是否会得出相似的结论。

# 4 Why are you interested in multimodal learning?

**英文题目：**

Why are you interested in multimodal learning?

**中文题目：**

为什么你对多模态学习感兴趣？

**English Answer:**

I'm interested in multimodal learning because real-world information usually comes from different sources. 

For example, in VQA tasks, the model needs to understand both vision and text. Also, if we want to detect a person's emotion, we can get complementary information from Brain signal, heart signal, and facial expression. 

So I think multimodal learning makes AI models much more applicable to real-world scenarios, and it definitely has great potential.

**中文参考回答：**  

我对多模态学习感兴趣，是因为现实世界中的信息通常来自不同的来源。

例如，在 VQA 任务中，模型需要同时理解视觉和文本。此外，如果我们想检测一个人的情绪，可以从脑信号、心电信号和面部表情中获取互补信息。

所以我认为多模态学习能让 AI 模型在实际场景中有更强的应用性，它绝对有很大的潜力。
