# 1 Why did you choose this programme?

**英文题目：**

Why did you choose the MSc in Computer Science programme at HKU?

**中文题目：**

为什么选择香港大学的计算机科学硕士项目？

**English Answer:**

The first reason I chose it is that HKU has a high ranking in computer science and it also has a strong academic reputation. I believe studying here can provide me with valuable academic resources and a good learning environment.

And the Second reason is that the programme matches my academic goals. I hope to strengthen my computer science foundations and research skills in artificial intelligence.

**中文参考回答：**

我选择这个项目主要有两个原因。

首先，港大在计算机科学领域排名较高，具有很强的学术声誉。我相信在这里学习能够为我提供丰富的学术资源和良好的学习环境。

其次，这个项目符合我的学术发展目标。我希望进一步巩固计算机科学基础以及人工智能的科研能力。

# 5 Why did you choose HKU instead of CUHK-Shenzhen?

**英文题目：**

Why did you choose HKU instead of CUHK-Shenzhen?

**中文题目：**

为什么选择香港大学，而不是香港中文大学（深圳）？

**English Answer:**

The first reason is that HKU has a longer history. I feel this gives it stronger connections with enterprises, which can provide us with more academic resources and better job opportunities. The second reason is that HKU has a better academic reputation. 

Of course, both schools are great, but if I had to choose one, I'd choose HKU.

**中文参考回答：**

第一个原因是港大的历史更悠久。我觉得这赋予了它更强的企业联系，能为我们提供更多的学术资源和更好的就业机会。第二个原因是港大的学术声誉更好。

当然，这两所学校都很棒，但如果必须选一个，我会选港大。
