# 1 What is the impact of AI on our daily lives?

**英文题目：**

What is the impact of artificial intelligence on our daily lives?

**中文题目：**

人工智能对我们的日常生活产生了什么影响？

**English Answer:**

I think AI has significantly changed our daily lives in many ways.

First, AI improves efficiency. For example, recommendation systems help us find relevant information, while AI assistants can help us complete tasks more quickly.

Second, AI has improved services in areas such as healthcare, education, and transportation.

However, AI also brings some challenges, such as privacy concerns, misinformation, and overdependence on technology.

Overall, I believe AI has a positive impact on society, but we need to use it responsibly and develop appropriate regulations.

**中文参考回答：**

我认为人工智能已经在很多方面显著改变了我们的日常生活。

首先，AI 提高了工作效率。例如，推荐系统能够帮助我们找到相关信息，而 AI 助手可以帮助我们更快地完成任务。

其次，AI 改善了医疗、教育和交通等领域的服务。

但是，AI 也带来了一些挑战，例如隐私问题、错误信息以及对技术的过度依赖。

总体来说，我认为 AI 对社会的影响是积极的，但我们需要负责任地使用它，并建立适当的监管机制。

# 2 What are the advantages and disadvantages of AI?

**英文题目：**

What are the main advantages and disadvantages of artificial intelligence?

**中文题目：**

人工智能有哪些主要优点和缺点？

**English Answer:**

AI has several important advantages.

First, it can process large amounts of data much faster than humans.

Second, it can automate repetitive tasks and improve productivity.

Third, AI can help people make better decisions by identifying patterns in complex data.

However, AI also has disadvantages.

**中文参考回答：**

人工智能有几个重要优点。

首先，它能够比人类更快地处理大量数据。

其次，它可以自动化重复性工作，提高生产效率。

第三，AI 能够从复杂数据中发现规律，辅助人们做出更好的决策。

不过，AI 也存在缺点。

它可能产生错误信息、输出有偏差的结果，并带来隐私风险。

另一个值得关注的问题是，人们可能过度依赖 AI，从而逐渐失去一部分独立解决问题的能力。

因此，我认为 AI 应该作为辅助人类的工具，而不是完全取代人类的判断。

# 3 What are the current problems of AI?

**英文题目：**

What are the major problems or challenges facing AI today?

**中文题目：**

目前人工智能面临哪些主要问题或挑战？

**English Answer:**

I think there are several major challenges facing AI today.

First, reliability is still a problem. AI models can sometimes generate incorrect or misleading information.

Second, data privacy and security are important concerns because AI systems often require large amounts of data.

Third, bias in training data may lead to unfair decisions.

Finally, some advanced AI models require significant computational resources, which increases development costs and energy consumption.

I believe future AI research should focus not only on improving model performance but also on reliability, fairness, and efficiency.

**中文参考回答：**

我认为目前人工智能主要面临几个挑战。

首先是可靠性问题。AI 模型有时会产生错误或具有误导性的信息。

其次是数据隐私和安全问题，因为 AI 系统通常需要大量数据。

第三，训练数据中的偏差可能导致不公平的决策。

最后，一些先进 AI 模型需要大量计算资源，这会增加开发成本和能源消耗。

我认为未来的 AI 研究不仅应该关注模型性能，还应该关注可靠性、公平性和效率。

# 4 How can we build a reliable AI system for a specific domain?

**英文题目：**

How can we build a reliable AI system for a specific domain?

**中文题目：**

如何为某个特定领域构建可靠的 AI 系统？

**English Answer:**

I think there are several important steps.

First, we need to collect high-quality and domain-specific data because data quality directly affects model performance.

Second, we should choose an appropriate model based on the requirements of the application.

Third, we need to evaluate the system using carefully designed test cases, including difficult and unusual situations.

For example, we can use retrieval-augmented generation, also known as RAG, to allow an AI model to retrieve information from reliable domain-specific documents before generating an answer.

We should also provide human supervision for high-risk decisions and continuously monitor the system after deployment.

In my opinion, reliability requires more than just a powerful model. It also depends on data quality, evaluation, and human oversight.

**中文参考回答：**

我认为主要包括几个步骤。

首先，我们需要收集高质量的领域专用数据，因为数据质量会直接影响模型性能。

其次，我们应该根据具体应用需求选择合适的模型。

第三，需要通过精心设计的测试案例评估系统，包括一些困难和特殊场景。

例如，我们可以使用检索增强生成技术，也就是 RAG，让 AI 模型在生成答案之前先从可靠的专业文档中检索信息。

对于高风险决策，我们还应该引入人工监督，并在系统部署后持续监控。

在我看来，可靠性不仅取决于模型是否足够强大，还取决于数据质量、系统评估和人工监督。

**重要专有名词：**

|English|中文|
|---|---|
|Domain-specific data|领域专用数据|
|Data quality|数据质量|
|Model evaluation|模型评估|
|Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)|检索增强生成|
|Human supervision|人工监督|
|Reliability|可靠性|
|Deployment|部署|

# 5 Will AI replace human jobs? What is your opinion?

**英文题目：**

AI is developing rapidly and may replace some human jobs. What is your opinion?

**中文题目：**

AI 发展非常迅速，可能取代一些人类工作，你怎么看？

**English Answer:**

I think AI will replace some repetitive and routine tasks, but it does not necessarily mean that it will replace all human jobs.

For example, AI can help with data analysis, document processing, and basic customer service.

However, many jobs require creativity, critical thinking, communication, and complex decision-making.

These abilities are still difficult to fully automate.

I believe AI will change the nature of many jobs rather than simply eliminate them.

Therefore, people should learn how to work with AI and continuously improve their skills.

**中文参考回答：**

我认为 AI 会取代一部分重复性和常规性的工作，但这并不意味着它会取代所有人类工作。

例如，AI 可以帮助完成数据分析、文档处理和基础客户服务。

但是，很多工作需要创造力、批判性思维、沟通能力以及复杂的决策能力。

这些能力目前仍然难以被完全自动化。

我认为 AI 更多的是改变许多工作的性质，而不是简单地消灭这些工作。

因此，人们应该学习如何与 AI 协作，并不断提升自己的能力。

# 6 What do you think AI cannot do?

**英文题目：**

What do you think AI cannot do well, or what are its limitations?

**中文题目：**

你认为 AI 有哪些无法很好完成的事情，或者有哪些局限性？

**English Answer:**

I think AI still has limitations in several areas.

First, it does not always understand the real-world context behind a problem.

Second, it may struggle with situations that are very different from its training data.

Third, it cannot independently take full responsibility for important decisions.

For example, in medical applications, AI can help doctors analyze information, but final decisions should still involve qualified medical professionals.

Therefore, I believe human judgment and responsibility remain essential.

**中文参考回答：**

我认为 AI 在几个方面仍然存在局限性。

首先，它并不总是能够真正理解问题背后的现实环境。

其次，当遇到与训练数据差异很大的场景时，它可能难以作出可靠判断。

第三，它无法独立承担重要决策所带来的全部责任。

例如，在医疗领域，AI 可以帮助医生分析信息，但最终决策仍然应该由专业医疗人员参与。

因此，我认为人类的判断和责任仍然不可或缺。

最后，AI 的发展可能加剧不平等，因为并不是所有人都能够平等地使用先进的 AI 技术。

# 12 What are the positive and negative effects of LLMs on programming?

**英文题目：**

What are the positive and negative effects of Large Language Models on programming?

**中文题目：**

大语言模型对编程有什么积极和消极影响？

**English Answer:**

I think Large Language Models have significantly changed the way people write code.

On the positive side, they can help developers generate code, explain unfamiliar concepts, find potential bugs, and write test cases.

This can improve development efficiency, especially for simple and repetitive tasks.

However, there are also some disadvantages.

LLMs may generate incorrect or insecure code, and developers may become too dependent on them.

Sometimes the generated code looks correct but may contain hidden problems.

Therefore, I believe developers should treat LLMs as assistants rather than replacements for programming knowledge.

They still need to understand the code, test it, and take responsibility for the final result.

**中文参考回答：**

我认为大语言模型已经显著改变了人们编写代码的方式。

积极方面，它们可以帮助开发者生成代码、解释不熟悉的概念、发现潜在错误以及编写测试用例。

这能够提高开发效率，尤其是处理一些简单和重复性任务时。

但是，也存在一些缺点。

LLM 可能生成错误或不安全的代码，而且开发者可能过度依赖它们。

有时生成的代码表面上看起来正确，但实际上可能存在隐藏问题。

因此，我认为开发者应该将 LLM 视为编程助手，而不是编程知识的替代品。

他们仍然需要理解代码、进行测试，并对最终结果负责。
