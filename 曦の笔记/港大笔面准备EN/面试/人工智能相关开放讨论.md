# 1 What is the impact of AI on our daily lives?

**英文题目：**

What is the impact of artificial intelligence on our daily lives?

**中文题目：**

人工智能对我们的日常生活产生了什么影响？

**English Answer:** 

As a CS student, I feel the impact of AI very directly. First, it makes learning much more efficient. For example, when I search for technical documentation or learn a new framework, AI tools can give me quick and clear answers. Second, it speeds up my programming. 

Of course, we shouldn't rely on it too much, but overall, it makes my daily study and work way more productive. 

**中文参考回答：**

作为一个计算机专业的学生，我非常直接地感受到了 AI 的影响。首先，它让学习变得更高效。例如，当我查阅技术文档或学习新框架时，AI 工具能快速给我清晰的答案。其次，它提升了我的编程速度。

当然，我们不应该过度依赖它，但总的来说，它让我的日常学习和工作变得高效得多。

# 2 What are the advantages and disadvantages of AI?

**英文题目：**

What are the main advantages and disadvantages of artificial intelligence?

**中文题目：**

人工智能有哪些主要优点和缺点？

**English Answer:**  

In my opinion, the biggest advantage of AI is that it boosts productivity. As a CS student, it helps me learn new frameworks and tools quickly and precisely, because it can search the internet and analyze datasets to give me comprehensive answers. It also speeds up my programming and helps me finish tasks much faster.

However, the main disadvantage is that we tend to rely on it too much instead of thinking by ourselves. If the AI makes a mistake, we might not catch it. And even if we do, we might lack the ability to fix it ourselves because we're so used to letting AI solve the problem for us.

**中文参考回答：**  

在我看来，AI最大的优点是它能提高生产力。作为一名CS学生，它能帮我快速且精准地学习新框架和工具，因为它能在互联网上搜索并分析数据集，给我全面的答案。它也能加速我的编程，帮我更快完成任务。

然而，主要的缺点是我们容易过度依赖它，而不是自己去思考。如果AI犯了错，我们可能根本发现不了。即使我们发现了，也可能缺乏独立解决它的能力，因为我们太习惯于让AI帮我们解决问题了。

# 3 What are the current problems of AI?

**英文题目：**

What are the major problems or challenges facing AI today?

**中文题目：**

目前人工智能面临哪些主要问题或挑战？

第二，许多 AI 系统依赖大量个人数据，这会增加数据滥用或泄露的风险。

# 4 How can we build a reliable AI system for a specific domain?

**英文题目：**

How can we build a reliable AI system for a specific domain?

**中文题目：**

如何为某个特定领域构建可靠的 AI 系统？

**English Answer:**  

First, I guess the most important aspect in constructing a reliable AI system is high-quality data. We must collect domain-specific data, because if the data is bad, even a powerful model won't work.

Second, strict testing and human oversight are also essential. We need to test the system with edge cases, and for high-risk decisions, humans must step in.

**中文参考回答：**  

首先，我认为构建可靠的 AI 系统最重要的一点是高质量的数据。我们必须收集特定领域的数据，因为如果数据不好，即使模型再强大也发挥不了作用。  

其次，严格的测试和人工监督也至关重要。我们需要用边缘/极端场景来测试系统，并且对于高风险决策，必须有人类介入。

# 5 Will AI replace human jobs? What is your opinion?

**英文题目：**

AI is developing rapidly and may replace some human jobs. What is your opinion?

**中文题目：**

AI 发展非常迅速，可能取代一些人类工作，你怎么看？

**English Answer:**  

I think AI will definitely take over repetitive and routine tasks, but it won't replace all human jobs. For example, jobs that need human creativity, communication, and complex decision-making are still hard for AI to fully automate.

So, I think AI will change how we work rather than just taking our jobs. We shouldn't be too afraid of it. Instead, we just need to learn how to use AI as a tool and improve our own skills.

**中文参考回答：**  

我认为 AI 肯定会接管重复性和常规性的任务，但它不会取代所有人类工作。例如，那些需要人类创造力、沟通能力和复杂决策的工作，AI 仍然很难完全自动化。  

所以，我认为 AI 更多的是改变我们的工作方式，而不是单纯地抢走我们的工作。我们不应该太害怕它，相反，我们只需要学会把 AI 当作工具来用，并提升自己的技能。

# 6 What do you think AI cannot do?

**英文题目：**

What do you think AI cannot do well, or what are its limitations?

**中文题目：**

你认为 AI 有哪些无法很好完成的事情，或者有哪些局限性？

In my opinion, AI can't replace humans in areas that need creativity, communication, and decision-making. Because AI mostly relies on existing data and experience, it's not really good at handling truly creative tasks.

Also, for important decisions like in the medical field, the **final call** should always be made by a human doctor, not an AI. AI can't take that responsibility.

**中文参考回答：**  

在我看来，AI 无法在需要创造力、沟通和决策的领域取代人类。因为 AI 主要依赖于现有的数据和经验，所以它并不擅长处理真正的创造性任务。  

此外，对于像医疗领域这样的重要决策，最终决定权应该始终由人类医生来掌握，而不是 AI。AI 无法承担这种责任。

# 7 What negative effects might AI bring to society?

**英文题目：**

What negative effects might AI bring to society?

**中文题目：**

人工智能可能给社会带来哪些负面影响？

**English Answer:**  

First, fake information. AI can easily generate very convincing but completely wrong information, which makes it hard to tell what is true.

Second, privacy risks. AI systems process a lot of our personal data, which can be dangerous if it's leaked.

Third, over-dependence. As I mentioned before, if we rely on AI too much, we might lose our own ability to think and solve problems.

**中文参考回答：**  
  
第一，假信息。AI 很容易生成看起来非常可信但完全错误的信息，让人很难分辨真假。  

第二，隐私风险。AI 系统会处理我们大量的个人数据，一旦泄露会非常危险。  

第三，过度依赖。就像我之前提到的，如果我们太依赖 AI，可能会丧失自己思考和解决问题的能力。  

# 8 After using AI, what do you think people might lose?

**英文题目：**

After using AI frequently, what do you think people might gradually lose?

**中文题目：**

频繁使用 AI 后，你认为人们可能逐渐失去什么？

**English Answer:**  

In my opinion, the biggest thing we might lose is our ability to think independently. For example, more and more people just ask AI directly when they face a problem. Even when AI gives them the answer, they don't think about how AI solved it. In this case, they will lose their thinking ability and creativity, because they just accept what AI gives them.

So, I think we should use AI properly to improve our efficiency. When we use it, we should think about how it got the answer and try to learn something new from it, rather than completely relying on it.

**中文参考回答：**  

在我看来，我们可能失去的最重要的东西是独立思考的能力。例如，越来越多的人遇到问题时直接去问 AI。即使 AI 给了他们答案，他们也不去思考 AI 是怎么解决的。这样一来，他们就会失去思考能力和创造力，因为他们只是接受了 AI 给的东西。  

所以，我认为我们应该正确使用 AI 来提高效率。当我们使用它时，我们应该思考它是如何得出答案的，并尝试从中学习新东西，而不是完全依赖它。

# 12 What are the positive and negative effects of LLMs on programming?

**英文题目：**

What are the positive and negative effects of Large Language Models on programming?

**中文题目：**

大语言模型对编程有什么积极和消极影响？

**English Answer:**

In my opinion, one of the biggest advantages of LLMs is that they can make programming faster and help us learn new things more quickly.

For example, when I need to learn a new framework or use a new tool, I can ask AI for help. It can quickly explain what the framework or tool does and why we should use it.

However, LLMs also have some disadvantages. For example, AI can sometimes make mistakes. If we simply accept its answers without thinking about why they are correct, we may fail to notice errors and even be misled.

So, I think we should treat AI as an assistant. When it gives us an answer, we should try to understand its reasoning and think about whether its solution is suitable for the problem.

**中文参考回答：**  

在我看来，大语言模型最大的优势之一是能够提高编程效率，并帮助我们更快地学习新知识。

例如，当我需要学习一个新的框架或者使用一个新的工具时，我可以向 AI 寻求帮助。它能够快速解释这个框架或工具的作用，以及我们为什么要使用它。

然而，大语言模型也有一些缺点。例如，AI 有时会犯错。如果我们只是接受它给出的答案，而不去思考答案为什么正确，我们可能就无法发现其中的错误，甚至会被误导。

所以，我认为我们应该把 AI 当作一个助手。当它给出答案时，我们应该尝试理解它的思路，并思考它提出的解决方案是否适合当前的问题。
